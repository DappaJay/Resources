AI Automation Cost Estimator

Model the full cost picture of replacing a role with AI automation - including build, running costs, support, emergency risk, and the customer churn most vendors never mention.

Why AI costs are different to traditional software costs. Traditional software is priced by seat or licence - predictable, budgetable, fixed. AI is priced by consumption - every token sent and received costs money, and that cost varies with how the system is built, how it is used, and how much traffic it handles. That is why this tool exists and why comparing scenarios matters more than relying on a single point estimate. The outputs are scenario estimates, not precise forecasts - use them to stress-test decisions before committing.

All default values are illustrative starting points only. Replace every field with figures from your own business before drawing any conclusions.
